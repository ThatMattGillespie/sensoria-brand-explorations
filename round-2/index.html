Refract teal, six ways
to treat images

Everything from Refract teal stays: warm paper and white surfaces, deep teal ink, amber and turquoise accents, Funnel Display (“Refract”) with the faint turquoise and amber fringe. The options differ only in how images are treated. D, E and F build on A.

  • New: full spectrumSeven hues at one lightness and chroma, so none outshouts the core teal and amber. A darker set for paper, a light set for deep teal.
  • Lighter speaker frameSpeakers stay in natural colour with only a light matte grade and a soft vignette. The old duotone filter is gone.
  • Real imagerySensoria’s event photos, the website illustrations, the nihonga brain and the Jeremy cut-out replace round 1’s stand-ins.
